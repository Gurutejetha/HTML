Top 5 Attractions

  • Grand Friday Mosque: Iconic architecture. Open 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. Free entry. Dress modestly.
  • National Museum: Cultural heritage. Open 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. Entry fee ₹250. Good to combine with a walk in Sulthan Park.
  • Artificial Beach: Urban relaxation. Open 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. Free entry. Evening times are cooler and colorful.
  • Underwater Dining: Unique dining experience. Open 11:00 AM - 10:30 PM. Cost ₹13,500 per 4 people. Ithaa Undersea Restaurant.
  • HP Reef: Diving paradise. Open 6:00 AM - 4:00 PM. Cost ₹6,000. Best visibility from December to May.
